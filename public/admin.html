QR labels to print

Labels are generic: they hold only a random code, no serial number and no project. Print a batch on sticker sheets, send them to a project, and link each label to equipment by scanning it in the app.

Print at 100 % scale. Scan one printed label with a phone before you print the whole batch. A new batch has new codes.
