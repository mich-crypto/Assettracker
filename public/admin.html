Locations

Turbines (WTG) need their surveyed position; the field app uses it to confirm where a technician is. The GPS radius is how close a phone must be to count as "at" the turbine. Vessels (SOV) need no position.

Import from CSV (name, lat, lon[, kind, radius_m])

One location per line. Existing names are updated. Example:
WTG A01,54.0123,6.5432
